Volunteer coordination program

Hermes Non-Profit

Hermes Non-Profit is a volunteer event coordination program operated by sole proprietor Travis Omernick. It uses a text-message system for coordinating volunteer events with volunteers.

Join volunteer SMS

Text JOIN to +1 (970) 470-8839 to enroll in recurring Hermes Non-Profit volunteer SMS coordination.

This website does not collect your phone number or SMS consent. You opt in only by sending JOIN from your own phone to +1 (970) 470-8839.

Text JOIN

By texting JOIN, you agree to receive recurring automated volunteer-coordination text messages from Hermes Non-Profit. Messages may include volunteer opportunities, event details, signup confirmations, follow-up questions, reminders, cancellation confirmations, and standby-opening offers.

Message frequency varies by participation. Participating volunteers receive 1–8 messages per event, plus replies to messages they initiate. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out of all messages or HELP for help.

Event cancellation is different from opting out

DROP <event keyword> cancels a specific event commitment. STOP unsubscribes the phone number from the entire Hermes Non-Profit SMS program.
