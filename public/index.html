Join the SMS test

Text JOIN

By texting JOIN, you agree to receive recurring automated volunteer-coordination text messages from Hermes Non-Profit. Messages may include test volunteer opportunities, event details, signup confirmations, follow-up questions, reminders, cancellation confirmations, and standby-opening offers.

Message frequency varies by participation. Most test participants receive 1–8 messages per test event, plus replies to messages they initiate. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out of all messages or HELP for help.

Event cancellation is different from opting out

When event features are active, DROP cancels a specific event commitment. STOP unsubscribes the phone number from the entire Hermes Non-Profit SMS program.
