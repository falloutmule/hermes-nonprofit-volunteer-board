SMS Terms and Conditions

These terms govern participation in the Hermes Non-Profit Volunteer SMS Testing Program.

1. Program description

Hermes Non-Profit is an internal volunteer-system testing project operated by sole proprietor Travis Omernick. The project is testing a text-message system for coordinating volunteer events. Messages may include test volunteer opportunities, event details, signup confirmations, follow-up questions, reminders, cancellation confirmations, and standby-opening offers. The program is currently limited to invited internal test participants.

2. Enrollment and consent

To enroll, review the public SMS disclosure and text JOIN to +1 (970) 470-8839. By texting JOIN, you consent to receive recurring automated volunteer-coordination text messages from Hermes Non-Profit at the mobile number used to enroll.

Participation is voluntary. Do not enroll a number that you do not own or have authority to use.

3. Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies by participation. Most test participants receive 1–8 messages per test event, plus replies to messages they initiate. Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier's messaging, data, roaming, and other charges remain your responsibility.

4. Opting out, help, and event cancellation

  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe from all Hermes Non-Profit text messages. You may receive one final confirmation of the opt-out.
  • Reply START to request re-enrollment after opting out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email Falloutmule@gmail.com.
  • When event features are active, DROP cancels a specific test event commitment. DROP does not unsubscribe you from the entire SMS program.

5. Standby offers

If a test event is full, a participant may be placed on a standby list. When a confirmed participant cancels, Hermes Non-Profit may offer the opening to one standby participant at a time. An offer is not guaranteed, may expire, and is not confirmed until the system sends an acceptance confirmation.

Hermes Non-Profit may limit or end access when necessary to protect participants, the service, or the testing process.

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on carrier networks, device availability, Twilio, internet services, and other systems outside Hermes Non-Profit's control. The program may be changed, interrupted, suspended, or discontinued during testing.

8. Privacy

Information is handled according to the Hermes Non-Profit Privacy Policy. Mobile phone numbers and messaging consent data are not shared, sold, rented, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
