Privacy Policy

Hermes Non-Profit is a volunteer event coordination program operated by sole proprietor Travis Omernick. This Privacy Policy explains how Hermes Non-Profit collects, uses, protects, and discloses information in connection with its public website and volunteer SMS coordination program.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Hermes Non-Profit volunteer SMS coordination program operated through +1 (970) 470-8839 and to the public pages describing that program. Participation is voluntary and requires SMS opt-in.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the volunteer program, we may collect or process:

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • Your name or other identifying information when you choose to provide it.
  • The text messages you send and the replies necessary to respond to them.
  • Event signup, cancellation, standby, and response information.
  • Consent, opt-in, HELP, opt-out, and re-enrollment records.
  • Message metadata such as timestamps, message identifiers, direction, and delivery status.
  • Limited technical information generated when you visit the public website.

The Volunteer Board is designed not to retain complete SMS message bodies in its primary database after processing. Operational logs exclude message bodies. Message content may remain with the messaging provider for delivery, troubleshooting, security, fraud prevention, or compliance.

3. How we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Coordinate volunteer events and participation.
  • Respond to messages and questions.
  • Manage volunteer opportunities, signups, cancellations, and standby offers.
  • Send requested confirmations, reminders, updates, and related replies.
  • Record and honor consent and opt-out choices.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, debug failures, and comply with applicable obligations.

4. Mobile information and consent are not shared for marketing

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may disclose information to service providers, including Twilio and hosting or infrastructure providers, only as necessary to transmit messages, host the service, secure the system, or otherwise operate the volunteer program. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with investigating misuse.

5. SMS frequency and charges

Message frequency varies by participation. Participating volunteers receive 1–8 messages per event, plus replies to messages they initiate. Message and data rates may apply. Hermes Non-Profit does not control charges imposed by your mobile carrier.

6. Your choices

  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe from all future Hermes Non-Profit texts.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.
  • After opting out, reply START to request re-enrollment.
  • Reply DROP <event keyword> to cancel a specific event without opting out of the whole program.

You may also contact Falloutmule@gmail.com to ask a privacy question or request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your number. We may retain limited suppression, consent, security, or legal records when necessary to honor an opt-out or meet an obligation.

7. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for event coordination, consent and opt-out records, security, troubleshooting, and applicable legal or operational requirements. Retention periods may differ by record type. Information held by service providers is also subject to their retention settings and policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to this volunteer program. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the program changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are published. If a change materially affects SMS consent or message use, we will update the public disclosure before relying on the changed practice.
